## Prompt

```text
Sculpted seed-bead and glitter relief artwork, wide 16:9 panoramic composition, every surface built from millions of tiny raised beads, granules and foam-clay florets, matte and tactile. Edge to edge, no frame, no border.

RIVER: a twisting blue river winds from the bottom edge up through the middle to a misty lake at the centre, its surface a mosaic of pale blue and turquoise bead-water dotted with scattered flat white pebble beads.

BANKS: the river is bordered by rolling sculpted banks and terraces, each a separate raised mass of tiny beads, shading through dusted rose, mauve, pale lilac, sage green, olive, ochre and warm tan, forming layered hills that step back into the distance.

LEFT TREES: tall slender trees with dark charcoal-bead trunks rise from the left bank, their canopies built from dense clusters of golden-yellow, apricot and orange beads, one large canopy swelling across the upper left corner.

RIGHT TREES: taller white trees dominate the right, their trunks the same dark bead-work, their crowns a dense cloud of pearl-white and cream beads, branches spreading across the upper right and reaching to the top edge.

DISTANCE: a wall of autumn forest in red, rust, orange and gold beads lines both sides of a pale misty clearing, dissolving into soft grey fog at its base.

CATHEDRAL: at the exact centre stands a white baroque cathedral with a tall ribbed central dome, a smaller dome on each flank, thin bell towers with cupolas, arched windows and gold bead finials on every spire, its silhouette soft-edged and glowing through the fog.

SKY: a pale silver-grey bead sky, slightly iridescent with faint lilac and peach blush low on the horizon behind the cathedral. Two small pale bead moons hang in the upper centre, one brighter, one faint.

PALETTE: pearl white, dusty rose, mauve, sage, ochre, apricot gold, rust orange, turquoise blue, charcoal. Beaded texture throughout, serene, dreamlike, clean and unlettered.
```
