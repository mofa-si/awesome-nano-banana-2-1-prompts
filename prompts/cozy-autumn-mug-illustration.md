## Prompt

```text
A cozy, modern still-life illustration of a hand holding a dark rust-colored ceramic mug filled with a warm seasonal drink, garnished with two bright orange slices, cinnamon sticks, and fresh evergreen sprigs. The hand and forearm enter the composition from the RIGHT side of the image, palm turned upward to support the mug. The person has deep burgundy nail polish and wears a burnt mustard-yellow sleeve with a small cream geometric check pattern and a dark olive cuff.

The background is a soft muted sage green with scattered translucent cream and pale golden circles suggesting gentle bokeh. Minimal, elegant composition with generous negative space, warm autumn and winter colors, tactile handmade charm, and a quiet cozy atmosphere.

Drawn entirely with alcohol art markers on lightly textured paper, featuring bold opaque marker strokes, visible overlapping color layers, subtle streaking, expressive hand-drawn outlines, slight variations in ink saturation, and crisp blocks of color with imperfect organic edges. Rich rust, burnt orange, burgundy, mustard, forest green, and sage palette. Contemporary editorial illustration, stylish hand-drawn artwork, simplified shapes, confident linework, beautifully rendered citrus and ceramic textures. Portrait composition, no text, no lettering, no watermark, no photorealism, no 3D rendering.
```
