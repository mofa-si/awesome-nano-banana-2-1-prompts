## Prompt

```text
A richly layered autumn flat-lay arrangement viewed directly from above, featuring miniature orange pumpkins, golden-yellow flowers, burnt-orange chrysanthemums, deep burgundy maple leaves, rusty red foliage, and scattered ochre leaves arranged across a deep charcoal-brown background. The objects are artfully scattered with a natural, organic rhythm, filling the composition from edge to edge. Pumpkins of different sizes are nestled between flowers and leaves, with delicate stems, softly defined ridges, and subtle variations in shape. Large autumn blooms feature layered petals with gently rounded edges, while maple leaves display sculptural veins and softly curled tips.

Rendered in a sophisticated soft matte 3D style, with smooth tactile surfaces, gently rounded forms, subtle ambient occlusion, soft diffused studio lighting, delicate contact shadows, and velvety non-reflective materials. The pumpkins resemble finely crafted matte ceramic or soft sculpted clay, the flowers have thick dimensional petals, and the leaves have softly embossed veins and gently raised edges. Beautifully restrained surface detail, smooth gradients of color, understated depth, and a premium handcrafted decorative-object aesthetic.

Color palette of vivid burnt orange, pumpkin orange, golden mustard, warm ochre, crimson, dusty burgundy, muted rust, and deep earthy brown. The dark background creates dramatic contrast and allows the warm autumn colors to glow. Balanced distribution of flowers, pumpkins, and foliage, with varied sizes and orientations, natural overlaps, no obvious grid, and no central focal object. Edge-to-edge seamless-looking composition with elements partially cropped along the borders, like an elegant seasonal decorative pattern.

Straight-down overhead camera angle, evenly distributed composition, soft cinematic illumination, matte finish, refined stylized 3D rendering, tactile sculptural details, cozy autumn atmosphere. No glossy plastic, no metallic surfaces, no photorealistic photography, no text, no lettering, no watermark.
```
