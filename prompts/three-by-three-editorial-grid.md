## Prompt

```text
3x3 photo grid showing various poses of the same man in a dramatic editorial portrait series. Confident man with dark wavy hair, thick beard, round black-tinted sunglasses, and a dark navy bomber jacket over a black shirt. Poses include: adjusting sunglasses, hand on chin thinking, arms crossed looking away, hand running through hair, head tilted down looking over sunglasses, and leaning against a dark wall. Intense red and orange studio backdrop with moody crimson shadows and strong rim lighting. Cinematic 85mm photography, high contrast, rich blacks, 1970s luxury fashion aesthetic.
> 
Option 2: Single Focus Pose Variation (Adjusting Sunglasses)
> Cinematic editorial portrait of a confident man with dark wavy hair and a full beard, wearing round vintage sunglasses and a navy bomber jacket. He is subtly lowering his sunglasses with one hand, looking directly into the camera with an intense, stoic expression. Close-up composition, dramatic red and orange background glow, strong rim light highlighting his hair and jacket collar. Deep shadows, rich color grading, shallow depth of field, 85mm lens, f/1.8, moody luxury fashion shoot.
> 
Option 3: Profile / Looking Away Pose
> Side-profile cinematic editorial portrait of a man with dark wavy hair, a thick beard, round black-tinted sunglasses, and a navy bomber jacket. He is looking off to the side with his hand thoughtfully on his chin. Deep crimson and orange atmospheric lighting behind him, creating a strong glowing contour along his profile and beard. High contrast, moody studio portrait, crisp focus, subtle film grain.
```
