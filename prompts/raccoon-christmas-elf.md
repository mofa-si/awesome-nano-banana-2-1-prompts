## Prompt

```text
Illustrate a hilariously untrustworthy raccoon dressed as a Christmas elf in the style of a vintage 1950s holiday comic. The raccoon wears a crooked green elf hat, has cookie crumbs all over its face, and is caught stealing a cookie from a plate while standing in a pile of torn wrapping paper. A long strand of Christmas ribbon is tangled around its tail like a sash. Give it a wide-eyed, completely unconvincing innocent expression. Use thick hand-inked outlines, expressive cartoon features, retro halftone shading, imperfect vintage print texture, and a cheerful palette of pine green, cherry red, mustard gold, and aged cream. The design should feel like a collectible old-fashioned Christmas novelty illustration with a clear central character, simple background, and bold readable shapes. No mockup, no watermark, no text.
```
