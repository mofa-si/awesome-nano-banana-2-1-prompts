## Prompt

```text
Create an ultra-realistic black-and-white editorial fashion portrait of a young man sitting confidently in a chair, photographed from a slightly low angle. He has thick, naturally curly, voluminous dark hair falling loosely over his forehead, a defined masculine face, a clean-shaven appearance, and a calm, serious expression with direct eye contact.

Pose and Expression: The subject sits in a relaxed, slightly reclined position with his legs apart. His right hand is raised toward his face, with his index finger resting vertically near the bridge of his nose and forehead, while his other fingers curve naturally in front of his mouth. His left hand rests casually on his upper thigh. His shoulders are relaxed, creating a thoughtful, mysterious, confident mood.

Outfit: He wears an oversized dark charcoal plaid blazer with subtle textured check patterns, broad structured shoulders, and classic notched lapels. Underneath is a fitted black high-neck turtleneck sweater. He wears light-wash, relaxed-fit denim jeans with visible seams and a black leather belt with a metallic buckle. The blazer sleeves feature small decorative buttons.

Composition and Camera: Vertical 3:4 portrait format, framed from slightly above the knees to the top of the head. The subject is centered and occupies most of the frame. Use a slightly low camera angle to emphasize the face, hands, shoulders, and oversized blazer. The foreground jeans appear prominent due to perspective. Include the visible armrests of a dark chair at the lower edges of the frame.

Lighting and Background: Dramatic natural side lighting from the upper left creates strong highlights on the curly hair, forehead, fingers, and textured clothing, with deep shadows across the opposite side of the face and blazer. The background is a plain, light-colored plaster wall with subtle irregular brush textures and imperfections. Use high-contrast monochrome photography with rich blacks, luminous grays, detailed skin texture, realistic fabric grain, and soft natural tonal transitions.

Photography Style: Luxury menswear editorial, cinematic black-and-white photography, minimalist studio portrait, moody fine-art aesthetic, realistic anatomy, natural skin pores, crisp hair details, authentic hand proportions, subtle film grain, deep tonal range, professional DSLR quality, 85mm portrait lens aesthetic, sharp facial focus, soft background detail, photorealistic, high resolution.

Important: Maintain a natural, unretouched appearance. Avoid exaggerated facial features, artificial skin smoothing, excessive sharpening, distorted fingers, extra fingers, warped hands, colorful elements, text, logos, watermarks, or unnecessary background objects.
```
