## Prompt

```text
1. Japan (deep indigo/red accent)
Vintage travel poster, tall portrait 4:5. Aged cream paper texture with grain and torn edges. Top: small spaced text "WELCOME TO", then huge distressed condensed sans-serif title "JAPAN" in deep red, with the tagline "LAND OF TRADITION, HARMONY & INNOVATION" below. Center: a sepia black-and-white halftone photo collage of Mount Fuji with a pagoda in front, a torii gate, a bullet train, and cherry blossom branches, with only the torii gate and train in red. Left side: a vertical Japanese title "日本" in brush-style type, a small Japanese flag, a red camellia illustration, and a small samurai-armor drawing. Right side: a vertical line of Japanese text, a small map outline of Japan, and a stamp badge reading "EST. 660 BC". Circular postmark stamp "TOKYO, JAPAN" with wavy cancellation lines. Footer: "LIVE THE PAST • ENJOY THE PRESENT • INSPIRE THE FUTURE" with small crown-style ornaments. Palette: cream, black, sepia, one red accent. Print-ready, crisp typography, no extra text.
2. France (navy blue accent)
Vintage travel poster, tall portrait 4:5. Warm ivory paper with creases and ink wear. Top: "BIENVENUE EN" in small spaced letters, then giant distressed condensed title "FRANCE" in navy blue, with the tagline "LAND OF ART, ELEGANCE & CULTURE". Center: a monochrome sepia collage featuring the Eiffel Tower rising through clouds, a Parisian café with bistro chairs, a Haussmann-style street, and the Arc de Triomphe, with a navy-blue vintage Citroën 2CV in the foreground as the only color element. Left side: vertical text "フランス", a small tricolor flag, a fleur-de-lis illustration, and a baguette-and-wine sketch. Right side: vertical French text, a small map outline of France, and a boxed badge "SINCE 1889 • PARIS". Round postmark "PARIS, FRANCE" with wavy lines. A cursive caption reads "Liberté, Égalité, Fraternité". Footer: "LIVE THE PAST • ENJOY THE PRESENT • INSPIRE THE FUTURE". Palette: ivory, black, sepia, navy accent. Retro print texture, sharp typography.
3. Egypt (terracotta/gold accent)
Vintage travel poster, tall portrait 4:5. Sand-colored aged paper with grain and stains. Top: "WELCOME TO", then huge weathered condensed title "EGYPT" in terracotta red, with the tagline "LAND OF PHARAOHS, MYSTERY & ETERNITY". Center: a sepia halftone collage of the Great Pyramids at sunset, a Sphinx, a felucca sailing on the Nile, and a camel caravan, with a golden pharaoh mask as the only accent color. Left side: vertical Arabic title "مصر", a small flag, a lotus flower illustration, and a small hieroglyph cartouche. Right side: vertical Arabic text, a small map outline of Egypt, and a stamp badge reading "CAIRO • CIVILIZATION SINCE 3100 BC". Circular postmark "CAIRO, EGYPT" with wavy cancellation lines. Footer: "LIVE THE PAST • ENJOY THE PRESENT • INSPIRE THE FUTURE". Palette: sand, black, sepia, terracotta, touches of gold. Crisp typography, vintage print finish.
```
