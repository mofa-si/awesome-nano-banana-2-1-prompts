## Prompt

```text
1. layered cut paper diorama, a colossal whale made of intricately folded origami paper swimming through a sky above a paper cut village of rooftops and windmills, dozens of paper layers creating depth, soft cast shadows between layers, tiny paper villagers flying paper kites in the foreground for scale, paper clouds with deckled edges, warm backlight glowing through thin paper, cream, dusty blue, terracotta and sage green palette, handcrafted, delicate, dreamy, massive scale

2. Art Nouveau poster illustration, a colossal moth empress with vast ornate wings spread over a gaslit belle epoque city at night, wings patterned with eyes, spirals and gold filigree, flowing sinuous linework, decorative floral border with whiplash curves, tiny elegant crowds on a stone bridge holding lanterns in the foreground, glowing moon halo behind her, flat color with gold metallic accents, plum, sage, ivory, antique gold and midnight teal palette, elegant, mystical, monumental scale

3. 1990s hand drawn anime cel animation still, a colossal white nine tailed fox spirit lying curled across green mountain ridges above a valley of rice terraces, its tails flowing like rivers of silk and cloud, glowing red markings on its face, towering cumulus clouds, a tiny girl in a straw hat with a bicycle on a country road in the foreground for scale, lush hand painted watercolor background, clean cel shading, summer afternoon light, emerald, sky blue, white and vermilion palette, nostalgic, magical, massive scale

4. medieval illuminated manuscript page, a colossal snail with an entire stone castle built on its shell crawling toward a tiny walled town, knights in chainmail with lances and shields charging it bravely in the foreground, flat medieval perspective, burnished gold leaf background, decorated initial letter and vine border with tiny strange creatures in the margins, aged vellum texture, cracked paint, lapis blue, vermilion, forest green, gold and parchment palette, whimsical, absurd, epic scale
```
