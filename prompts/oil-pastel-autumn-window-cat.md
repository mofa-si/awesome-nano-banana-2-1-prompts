## Prompt

```text
A cozy autumn afternoon, viewed from behind a woman sitting on the floor beside a tall window, quietly enjoying a warm drink while her cat sits beside her, both gazing out at the fall scenery. The woman has shoulder-length auburn hair, wears a soft cream oversized sweater, rust-colored leggings, and warm tan socks. A brown tabby-and-white cat sits close beside her, its tail curled comfortably around its paws.

Outside the window, a large golden-yellow tree with loose, windswept branches sheds amber, ochre, and muted red leaves. A few pumpkins rest near the windowsill, and a simple strand of warm white round bulb fairy lights drapes casually along the window frame. Soft autumn sunlight filters through the glass, casting gentle, imperfect shadows across the floor.

Rendered in expressive oil pastel artwork on textured heavyweight paper, with visible pastel strokes, layered pigment, softly smudged edges, broken color, and tactile chalky texture. Warm earthy palette of burnt sienna, rust, ochre, golden mustard, cream, olive green, and charcoal. Intimate, peaceful, slightly nostalgic mood, charming imperfect details, artistic mark-making, beautiful composition, rich tactile surfaces, handmade fine-art feel. Portrait-oriented composition, no text, no lettering, no watermark.
```
