## Prompt

```text
2x2 grid, 16:9, do this for physics: INPUT: $ SUBJECT

A = {
  bold educational infographic,
  segmented comic layout,
  hand-lettered headers,
  bright color zones,
  doodles, arrows, icons,
  speech bubbles,
  explainer-chart readability
}

B = {
  minimalist editorial cartoon,
  simple black-and-white line art,
  symbolic characters and objects,
  deadpan humor,
  clean visual jokes,
  strong readability,
  newspaper-strip clarity
}

C = {
  cinematic 3D concept illustration,
  cute stylized figures,
  glossy rounded props,
  emotional facial expressions,
  staged metaphor scenes,
  high-contrast lighting,
  bold signage and slogan blocks
}

SUBJECT_CORE = stylize($ SUBJECT)
where:
- preserve the core meaning and symbolic identity of $ SUBJECT
- reduce it into immediately readable characters, props, metaphors, or systems
- make the concept understandable at a glance
- allow the scene to feel both informative and satirical

INFO_STRUCTURE = infer_explainer_system($ SUBJECT)
where:
- create one main headline idea
- surround it with labeled subpoints, warning signs, benefits, trade-offs, or commentary
- use boxes, banners, lists, arrows, side notes, and small callouts
- include readable symbolic interactions between figures, objects, and ideas

VISUAL_FUSION:
- core figures rendered in cartoon language with thick outlines
- some props or focal characters rendered as soft 3D toy-like objects
- informational framework rendered like a comic infographic
- humor rendered through ironic labels, visual metaphors, and contrast between cute form and serious meaning

COLOR_RULES:
- strong poster palette
- bright yellow, red, cyan, blue, green, white, and black
- optional grayscale area for comic-strip contrast
- avoid muddy realism or muted corporate palettes

OUTPUT:
A hybrid concept-poster for $ SUBJECT that combines infographic clarity, editorial satire, comic-strip readability, and toy-like 3D metaphor staging.
```
