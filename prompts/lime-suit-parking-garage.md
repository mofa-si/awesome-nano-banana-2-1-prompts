## Prompt

```text
Create a single photorealistic fashion editorial image arranged as a precise 2×2 grid of four equally sized photographs, separated by thin, clean white gutters. Overall square format. Each panel depicts the SAME beautiful adult woman, age 25, in a different extreme but anatomically believable pose.

CHARACTER AND WARDROBE LOCK:
The exact same woman appears in all four panels: an oval face, defined cheekbones, almond-shaped brown eyes, straight dark eyebrows, a small straight nose, full natural lips and a sleek dark pixie haircut. Natural skin texture and understated editorial makeup. She wears the exact same oversized acid-green tailored suit, black fitted tank top, chunky black leather loafers and small silver hoop earrings in every panel. Maintain identical facial structure, haircut, body proportions, clothing colours and accessories throughout. Expressions change, identity does not.

SHARED SETTING:
An underground parking garage with concrete columns, painted white parking lines and overhead fluorescent strip lights. Consistent cool ambient lighting, restrained warm light in the distance, believable shadows and realistic fabric colours. The four photographs belong to the same fashion campaign.

TOP LEFT — THE SHOE-FIRST SQUAT:
She drops into a deep, wide squat with both feet firmly planted, knees opening naturally outward. Her torso leans forward between her thighs. One elbow rests on its corresponding knee while the other hand loosely grips her jacket lapel. Her right loafer sits very close to the lens, oversized in the foreground. Chin lowered, she looks upward into the camera with a wicked asymmetric grin and one eyebrow raised.
Camera at floor level, 16mm ultra-wide lens angled upward. Keep her face unobstructed between the knees, with a clearly readable connection between hips, legs and feet.

TOP RIGHT — THE SIDEWAYS HUMAN KNOT:
She sits sideways on a low concrete parking barrier, pelvis fully supported. Her left foot rests firmly on the ground while her right knee is lifted toward her chest, right foot resting on the barrier. She bends her torso sideways to her left, plants her left palm on the barrier beside her hip, and curves her right arm over her head. Her face looks toward the lens beneath the raised arm, framed by the open space between elbow and shoulder. Mouth open in a fierce, spontaneous laugh.
Low three-quarter camera position, 20mm wide lens, slight Dutch tilt. Show the supporting hand, seated hips and grounded foot so the unusual pose remains physically understandable.

BOTTOM LEFT — THE OVERHEAD SPRAWL:
She lies on her back on clean concrete, her jacket spread beneath her shoulders. Her left leg extends diagonally across the frame, while her right knee bends outward with the right foot flat on the ground. Her left arm extends away from the body, palm relaxed. Her right hand reaches straight upward toward the overhead camera, fingers naturally spread, positioned beside rather than over her face. She gives the lens a theatrical mock gasp, eyes wide, lips parted with a hint of amusement.
Direct overhead 20mm shot, full body visible. The reaching hand appears larger through proximity while her face remains clearly readable. White parking lines create a graphic composition around her body.

BOTTOM RIGHT — THE FORWARD-FOLD AMBUSH:
She stands with feet wider than hip width and knees softly bent, folding forward at the hips toward the camera. Her left palm rests on her left thigh for support. Her right hand reaches toward the lens in a playful open-handed attempt to grab it. Her head lifts just enough for direct eye contact, neck naturally aligned. A crooked triumphant grin, nose slightly scrunched, one eye narrowed.
Camera low and close, 18mm wide lens aimed upward. The reaching hand occupies one side of the foreground, not the centre, leaving her entire face visible. Keep enough of her hips and both legs in frame to explain the forward fold.

PHOTOGRAPHIC FINISH:
Authentic high-fashion photography, lively imperfect timing, detailed skin, individual hair strands, visible fabric weave, believable suit folds and realistic leather. Bold perspective comes from camera placement and relative distance, never from stretched anatomy. Crisp expressive eyes, coherent lighting and contact shadows. Each panel has a distinct silhouette, expression and composition.

LAYOUT AND ANATOMY CONSTRAINTS:
Exactly four panels. Exactly one instance of the same woman per panel. Four distinct poses, no repeated composition. No body parts crossing between panels. Exactly two arms and two legs per woman, anatomically coherent hands, connected shoulders and hips, plausible joint angles. Preserve the same face even when expression and camera angle change.

No extra people, identity drift, outfit changes, duplicated limbs, fused fingers, dislocated joints, stretched faces, plastic skin, CGI rendering, illustration, captions, lettering, logos or watermarks.
```
