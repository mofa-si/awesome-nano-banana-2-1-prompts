## Prompt

```text
2x2 grid, 16:9, do this for 4 famous manga: Isometric 3D diorama where the word "[$WORD]" IS the landscape: each giant 3D letter is transformed into a miniature building, habitat, or scene built from the world of [$SUBJECT] (the model infers per-letter details). Tiny inhabitants and props from the subject live on and inside the letters. Reads as a word from afar, as a bustling micro-city up close. A motto on a plinth beneath. Tilt-shift, soft light, seamless white background, ultra-detailed, legible text
```
