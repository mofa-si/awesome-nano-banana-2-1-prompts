## Prompt

```text
A spooky, richly detailed vintage Halloween storybook illustration. Across the top, clearly display the exact text: “When black cats prowl and pumpkins gleam, may luck be yours on Halloween.” Below it, groups of sleek black cats prowl down a cobblestone street toward the viewer, framed by charming vintage homes and towering gnarled trees. Glowing jack-o’-lanterns line the sidewalks, casting warm amber light against cool moonlit shadows; add a thin veil of mist, dramatic silhouettes, intricate textures, and an atmospheric, whimsical-yet-eerie mood. Balanced poster composition, crisp focal details, cohesive antique color palette.
```
